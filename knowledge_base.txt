HOSPITAL OPERATIONS & PATIENT EXPERIENCE
RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version 1.0

PURPOSE
This knowledge base supports an AI hospital customer-support system.
It provides reference information for identifying, understanding, categorizing,
and responding to patient and attendant complaints related to hospital
operations, facilities, staff, communication, treatment delays, food,
billing, counseling, housekeeping, transportation, and patient experience.

The AI must use this knowledge base as its primary reference.
The AI must not invent hospital policies, timelines, charges, clinical decisions,
or commitments that are not present in the knowledge base.

==================================================
1. CANTEEN / FOOD COMPLAINTS
==================================================

Common complaints:
- Special dietary requirements were not followed.
- Food does not match the patient's prescribed diet.
- Wrong food was delivered.
- Food quality is poor.
- Food is served cold.
- Food is not served on time.
- Food is delayed.
- Food quantity is insufficient.
- Attendant food is unavailable or delayed.
- Patient or attendant is unhappy with the taste or quality.
- Food tray is not collected on time.

Possible complaint category:
FOOD / CANTEEN

Expected handling:
1. Acknowledge the patient's concern.
2. Verify the patient's location/room and meal requirement.
3. Check whether the complaint is related to food quality, timing,
   quantity, delivery, or diet compliance.
4. Coordinate with the Dietitian/Canteen/Food Services team as applicable.
5. Record the complaint and action taken.
6. Escalate if the issue remains unresolved.

The AI must not provide medical dietary advice.
Diet-related clinical decisions should be referred to the Dietitian/clinical team.

17. GENERAL PATIENT COMPLAINT HANDLING
==================================================

Every complaint should be handled using the following approach:

1. IDENTIFY
Determine what the patient is complaining about.

2. CATEGORIZE
Assign the complaint to the appropriate category:
- Food/Canteen
- Dietitian
- Referral
- Facility
- Staff Behavior
- Treatment Delay
- Food Delay
- Customer Support
- Transportation
- Housekeeping
- Medical Counseling
- Payment Counseling
- Billing
- Admission Kit
- Room Amenities
- Communication Gap

3. VERIFY
Check the available information before providing an answer.

4. COORDINATE
Identify the appropriate department/team responsible for resolution.

5. UPDATE
Provide the patient/attendant with the latest confirmed status.

6. ESCALATE
Escalate when the issue cannot be resolved using available information.

7. CLOSE
Record the action taken and confirm resolution when possible.

18. AI RESPONSE RULES
==================================================

The AI should:

- Be polite and professional.
- Show empathy toward the patient/attendant.
- Keep responses concise.
- Personalize the response using available patient/email information.
- Never blame staff or departments without verified information.
- Never invent hospital policies.
- Never invent charges.
- Never provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Never promise an unconfirmed resolution time.
- Use the knowledge base as the first source.
- Use memory/history when available.
- Assign a confidence score to every generated answer.
- If confidence is below the required threshold, send the query for
  validation/web research.
- Clearly distinguish verified information from assumptions.
- Escalate clinical, financial, safety, or unresolved complaints
  to the appropriate hospital team.


==================================================
19. CONFIDENCE SCORING
==================================================

Confidence should be based on how clearly the available knowledge supports
the answer.

90-100%:
Information directly matches the knowledge base and the answer is clear.

70-89%:
Information is mostly supported but some clarification may be required.

Below 70%:
Knowledge base does not provide sufficient information.
The answer should not be treated as reliable without additional validation.

The system should use:

CONFIDENCE >= 70%
→ Send to Validation Agent

CONFIDENCE < 70%
→ Perform external/web validation
→ Re-evaluate confidence
→ Retry/refine if necessary
